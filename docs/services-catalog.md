# Default services catalog

Seeded for every new shop by `shop_type` (men / women / kids; a shop can enable several).
The owner edits names, **prices (EGP)** and durations. Durations below are Claude's estimates, not market data: confirm with real shops in the pilot. Prices are intentionally NOT pre-filled: the owner sets them, and the customer sees them before joining.
