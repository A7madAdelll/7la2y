# Proposal Review — Barbershop Virtual Queue

Review of the investment proposal dated Oct 31, 2026. Findings are ranked by severity.

## Blockers (fix before sending)

1. **No ask.** Section 9 (team, amount, funding type, use of funds, milestones) is empty. Decide grant/accelerator (e.g. Flat6Labs, Falak, ITIDA) vs. equity; without traction, a grant or accelerator likely fits better.
2. **Unit economics unproven and possibly negative.** EGP 100–200/month is a few dollars. A busy shop can trigger ~1,500+ WhatsApp alerts/month (30 customers/day × 2 alerts × 30 days); billed per message, this may exceed revenue.
   - ~~Customer-messages-first makes replies free~~ — **no longer true from Oct 1, 2026.** See [whatsapp-cost.md](whatsapp-cost.md): $0.0036/message; fix is fewer alerts (one, remote joiners only).
   - Add a table: price, WhatsApp cost/shop, hosting, acquisition cost/shop, gross margin, break-even shop count.
3. **No evidence.** All validation is planned, none done. Run the 10 owner interviews first and put the results in the Problem section.
4. **Problem not quantified.** Add the number of barbershops (Egypt / target city, with method), and walk-aways/day × average ticket = lost revenue/month per shop.

## Strengthen

- **Lead with revenue, not crowd control.** Put the loyalty card and "recover walk-aways" in the executive summary.
- **Weak sources.** The Zenoti "only platform" line is vendor marketing; the Fresha "19 barbers" figure needs a date.
- **Qme is a threat, not a gap.** Explain why a $3M-funded queue company won't move down-market.
- **"Network effects" is overstated.** Barber referrals are word of mouth; call it referral-driven distribution.
- **Go-to-market needs numbers:** shops visited/week, trial-to-paid rate, cost per shop acquired.
- **Roadmap needs dates and costs per phase** so funding milestones map onto it.

## Missing risks

- **Payment collection:** InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, Fawry, or cash via field rep.
- **Data protection:** customer phone numbers fall under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (151/2020).
- **WhatsApp Business API approval / Meta business verification** can delay launch.
- **Owners may like visible crowds** (a full shop is social proof).
- **Reorder vs. fairness:** drag-to-reorder for regulars, shown on the waiting-room TV, can recreate "who came first" disputes.

## Not verified

Cited sources and Meta's current WhatsApp pricing for Egypt have not been checked. Verify message cost first.
